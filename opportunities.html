Research Fellowships

We are available to support applications for various fellowship schemes including (but not limited to):

  • Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC) - Fellowships
  • Leverhulme Trust - Early Career Research Fellowships
  • Royal Academy of Engineering - Research Fellowships

Please contact us with your CV and a brief research proposal if you are interested in applying.
